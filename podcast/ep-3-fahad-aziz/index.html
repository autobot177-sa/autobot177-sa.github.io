Welcome to the StartupStudios.co Podcast, featuring people who build startups!

This week, Raj and Saif interview Fahad Aziz, the co-founder of Caremerge, a healthtech startup in Silicon Valley which was acquired in 2022. Fahad tells us about growing up in Pakistan, finding early success with tech startups while in college, and the experiences which ultimately led him to moving to the US and starting his own startup.
