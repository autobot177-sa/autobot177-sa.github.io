Welcome to the StartupStudios.co Podcast, featuring people who build startups!

This week, Raj and Saif interview Young Han.

Young is a serial entrepreneur, advisor, and investor with more than 20 years of success in creating and implementing strategic operations plans to solve growth challenges. Previously based in Silicon Valley, Young played an instrumental role at hyper-growth venture-backed companies like Philz Coffee and Limelight Health; he wrangled the masses at Starbucks and Apple; and he has since built a myriad of businesses, including Forever Young Agency.

Thank you Young for sharing your story!
