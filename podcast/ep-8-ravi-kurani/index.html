Welcome to the StartupStudios.co Podcast, featuring people who build startups!

This week, Raj and Saif interview Ravi Kurani.

Ravi is the founder & CEO of Sutro, a smart pool water monitoring system out to, in his own words, “solve water!” Recognized by Forbes “30u30” in the energy category in 2017, Ravi shares the story about his upbringing, working in the family business, to supporting socially conscious startups and entrepreneurs as an analyst at Village Capital. A must listen for early-stage founders and investors about taking calculated risks on people and ideas.

Thank you Ravi for sharing your story!
