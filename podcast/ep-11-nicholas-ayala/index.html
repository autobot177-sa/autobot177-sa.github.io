Ep 11 - Nicholas Ayala

This week, Raj and Saif interview Nicholas Ayala!

Nick shares his journey and insights as a college student exploring various ventures, to co-founding his first startup QMi, to building operations at early-stage startups like Clubhouse, Legion Technologies, and Fair Square Medicine. Nicholas talks about the challenges faced by first-generation entrepreneurs and emphasizes the significance of small wins in motivating a team. His experiences offer valuable insights into the early days of startups and the lessons learned along the way.

Thank you Nicholas  for sharing your story with us! Watch or listen wherever you prefer at www.startupstudios.co/podcast/ep-11-nicholas-ayala
