Hear from StartupStudios.co co-founder & co-host Raja Mawad about moving to the US as an immigrant, finding success in finance, and then pivoting to health & wellness tech in Seattle with Thrv.Health.

Welcome to the StartupStudios.co Podcast, featuring people who build startups!

This week, StartupStudios.co co-founder & co-host Raja Mawad shares his story and journey as a young immigrant moving to Houston. We discuss what it was like breaking the mold his parents expected him to conform to, leading to a successful career as an investment banker and fund manager. When his fund was accused of fraud, Raj spent years fighting to prove his innocence – he was finally redeemed in 2022. It was during that difficult period when Raj rebuilt his career, pulling from his experience as a Division 1 collegiate athlete to launch his own brick & mortar health & wellness business through acquisition in 2021.

After selling his last business, Raj founded Thrv.Health, a healthtech marketplace startup in Seattle that matches trainers with customers looking for custom wellness solutions. Raj is also the founder of Mad-Hat.co, an accelerator program he launched to assist early-stage founders with capital raising and go-to-market launch support. Mad-Hat.co has worked with 33 startups in the last few years, with >70% using Raj’s proven method to successfully launch and raise seed funding. Raj continues working with founders through StartupStudios.co.

Thank you Raj for sharing your story!
