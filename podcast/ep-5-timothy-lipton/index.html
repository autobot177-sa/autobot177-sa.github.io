Welcome to the StartupStudios.co Podcast, featuring people who build startups!

This week, Raj and Saif interview Timothy Lipton.

Timothy is a financial advisor with more than 15 years of success advising early-stage startups from their napkin stage to series-B. Often founders at this early stage will delay finance and accounting compliance to a later stage when they can afford to hire a larger firm. At Momentum Finance, TL works with teams to stay compliant, get a better snapshot of their business’s health, receive fractional CFO / back-office support, and prove to later-stage investors that the team is ready for the big leagues. Previously based in Silicon Valley, Timothy is now based in Utah and actively engaged in the Salt Lake City startup ecosystem.

Thank you Tim for sharing your story!
