About

The operator behind the agency.

StartupStudios is led by Saif Akhtar, a founder-operator based in San Jose, California. He doesn’t teach GTM from the sidelines — he runs it every week inside his own companies, then installs what works inside yours.

SA

Saif Akhtar

Founder, StartupStudios · Co-founder & COO, Horizon Labs · Founder, SimplyHRA

Mechanical engineer turned operator (SJSU ’11). Saif has spent his career on the commercial side of hard problems: selling into regulated HR/benefits buying cycles, building two-sided marketplaces, and running outbound himself — not delegating it to a playbook he never tested.

The companies he runs

Two laboratories, one playbook.

Horizon Labs — co-founder & COO

A Sharetribe marketplace development agency, run with technical co-founder Sinan Sari. Two-sided marketplaces, shipped product, real customers — the hard kind of GTM, where both sides of the market have to show up.

SimplyHRA — founder

A bootstrapped ICHRA benefits-admin SaaS, founded 2019: 40 employers and ~400 members at $29 per employee per month. Saif personally runs the full go-to-market motion — broker outreach, lifecycle email, SEO and content, partnerships — inside a regulated buying cycle with long sales loops and skeptical buyers.

How we work

Three rules.

  • Senior-only. The person on your kickoff call is the person doing the work. No junior handoffs.
  • Stated, not spun. Results are labeled with exactly how verified they are. We’d rather underclaim than overclaim.
  • Operators, not theorists. Every playbook we sell has already survived contact with a real P&L — usually ours.
